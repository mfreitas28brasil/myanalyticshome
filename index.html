About

I'm Matheus Freitas, a Senior Data Analyst with 9+ years of experience, currently on the UX Research team at Epic Games, where I turn player research data into actionable insights and build the data and visualization infrastructure behind that work.

I specialize in Tableau Desktop and Prep, with a track record across financial analysis (P&L, forecast, revenue, gross margin), sales, user research and people analytics, for clients in the US, France and Brazil.

Lately my work has shifted toward AI-native analytics: I use Claude Code and Codex as day-to-day engineering partners to build analytics products in HTML — interactive reports and dashboards backed by real data pipelines — and I design the reusable skills, harnesses and repository standards that make that work reliable and repeatable across a team.
